# Blocks Puzzle Daily

A daily sliding block puzzle that lives right inside Reddit.

## How It Works
- A new puzzle every day
- Slide the blocks out of the way to free the red one
- Use as few moves as you can and try to beat par
- Play right in your Reddit feed, no download needed

## Compete
- Daily leaderboard, see how you rank against other players
- Build a streak by solving every day
- Share your score in the post's comments, with an optional note
- Browse and play previous dailies

## Your Data
- Only your Reddit username and your game results are used
- No emails, passwords, or personal details are collected
- Nothing is sold or shared
