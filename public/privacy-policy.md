# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 20, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Blocks Puzzle Daily collects, uses, and protects your information when you play on Reddit.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Reddit Username:** Used to identify you and show leaderboards.
- **Puzzle Results:** Moves, solve time, and which daily puzzles you have solved.
- **Streaks:** Your current and best daily streak.
- **Timing Data:** When you start a puzzle, so times are fair.
- **Avatar Link:** Your public Reddit avatar image link, saved so it can be shown on leaderboards.
- **Comments You Choose to Post:** If you press the share button, your result and optional note are posted as a Reddit comment from your account.

We do **not** collect email addresses, passwords, location, or any sensitive personal information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To run and improve the game
- To show daily leaderboards, ranks, and streaks
- To post your result comment, only when you ask us to
- To detect and prevent cheating

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except as required by law or Reddit's platform policies. Your username, score and avatar are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Comments you post are public on Reddit.

## 4. Data Storage
Data is stored using Reddit's Devvit storage. Only the developers have access.

## 5. Cookies and Tracking
We do not use cookies or third-party trackers.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The game is for users 13 and older, in line with Reddit's policies.

## 7. Your Choices
- You can play without posting any comment.
- You can delete comments you posted directly on Reddit.
- To ask about or request removal of your game data, contact us using the details below.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted in this document.

## 9. Contact
For privacy questions, contact the developers through Reddit modmail or the project's GitHub repository.
